She came with a stack of words.

“What if we made something she could actually use?”

Instead of paper + phonetic spelling, I built an interactive way for her daughter to practise the words with audio.

03 · EXPERIENCE

Activity

04 · LIMITS

Constraints

Time, number of students, devices, language, etc.

Example: “Build a simple interactive activity for A2 students to practise asking for directions. Students should choose answers, get immediate feedback, and repeat the task with new examples. Keep the language simple and make it work on a phone.”
